# Dice

## Values
- A die has six faces
- The faces are numbered one through six

## Identity
- A player is identified by the appearance of their dice
- Each player's dice appearance is distinct within a match
- A player's name is available on demand
- The usual display shows dice rather than names
- A colour chosen by the human is not used for any other player
- Opponents' dice colours maximise the smallest difference between any two players' colours
