# Dice

## Geometry
- A die has six faces
- The faces are numbered one through six
- A die is a cube intersected with a sphere
- Opposite faces sum to seven
- A die is right-handed

## Appearance
- A die has a face texture
- A die has a glyph texture
- A die has a bevel texture
- A texture supports speckles
- A texture supports a rough three dimensional appearance
- A texture supports glossiness
- A texture supports flatness
- The glyph for the one face may differ in kind from the glyphs for the other faces

## Identity
- A player is identified by the appearance of their dice
- Each player's dice appearance is distinct within a match
- A player's name is available on demand
- The usual display shows dice rather than names
