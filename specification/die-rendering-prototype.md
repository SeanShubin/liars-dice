# Die Rendering Prototype

## Scope
- The prototype renders one die
- The die can be viewed from any angle
- The die is rotated by panning
- The prototype renders the ground

### Settings
- Every setting can be changed while the prototype runs
- The face texture is a setting
- The bevel texture is a setting
- The glyph texture is a setting
- Each texture effect is a setting
- The ground appearance is a setting

## Out of Scope
- The prototype has no game rules
- The prototype has no claims
- The prototype has no network
- The prototype keeps no event log
- The prototype does not animate a roll

## Acceptance
- Every dice appearance in the specification can be seen in the prototype
