# Minimal Game

## Scope
- The minimal game is two dimensional
- One human plays against AI opponents
- The human chooses the colour of their dice
- The human chooses the number of opponents
- The number of opponents is at least one
- Every number the human chooses can be increased and decreased
- Every interaction is a single touch
- The human can reach every legal claim
- An invalid claim cannot be made
- A die is shown as its value in its owner's colour
- The human sees the probability that a prospective claim is true
- A probability accounts only for how many dice are unknown and how faces are distributed
- A probability does not account for any player's behaviour
- Every claim of the current round is listed
- A listed claim shows the claim
- A listed claim shows how many dice the claimer held

## Out of Scope
- The minimal game has no network
- The minimal game has no human opponents
- The minimal game does not represent dice as physical objects
- The minimal game has no textures
- The minimal game has no ground

## Acceptance
- A match can be played from start to finish by touch alone
