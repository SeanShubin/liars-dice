# Minimal Presentation

## Scope
- The minimal presentation is two dimensional
- Every gameplay interaction is a single touch
- Every number the local player chooses can be increased and decreased
- The local player can reach every legal claim
- An invalid claim cannot be made
- A die is shown as its value in its owner's colour
- The local player sees the probability that a prospective claim is true
- A probability accounts only for how many dice are unknown and how faces are distributed
- A probability does not account for any player's behaviour
- Every claim of the current round is listed
- A listed claim shows the claim
- How many dice each player holds is shown

## Out of Scope
- The minimal presentation does not represent dice as physical objects
- The minimal presentation has no textures
- The minimal presentation has no ground

## Acceptance
- A match can be played from start to finish by touch alone
